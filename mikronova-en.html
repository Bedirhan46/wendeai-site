Mikronova – Explore inside the cell!

An ad-free educational science game for kids aged 5–9: with Mikronish and her friends, children discover what happens inside a cell – through audio stories, jigsaw puzzles, coloring pages and a quiz.

Free on Google Play

✅ Teacher Approved on Google Play

9 organelles, 100+ activities

  • Mitochondria – the cell's power plant
  • Ribosome – the protein factory
  • Peroxisome – the hospital that cleans up harmful substances
  • Lysosome – the cleaning and recycling center
  • Golgi Apparatus – the cargo center
  • Endoplasmic Reticulum – the cell's road network
  • Vacuole – the water and food storage
  • Cell Membrane – the smart door
  • Chloroplast – the photosynthesis factory of plant cells

Centrosome and Nucleus chapters are coming soon!

What kids will find in Mikronova

📖 Audio stories
Every organelle has its own narrated story – great for kids who can't read yet.
🧩 Jigsaw puzzles
5 colorful puzzles per chapter with big pieces for little hands.
🎨 Coloring pages
Large areas that are easy to color with a finger.
❓ Quiz
Short quizzes at the end of every chapter reinforce what was learned.
🌍 3 languages
English, German and Turkish.

For parents and teachers

  • No ads, no in-app purchases
  • No personal data collected – progress stays on the device
  • YouTube videos only after a parental question
  • Content for preschool and elementary school, a great companion for science lessons
